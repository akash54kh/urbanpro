Fractions Teaching Session
Akash taught Mohammed about fractions by explaining concepts using examples from their textbook. They discussed how fractions represent parts of a whole, with examples of sharing one roti among different numbers of children. Akash explained the relationship between numerators and denominators, noting that when the numerator is the same but the denominator increases, the fraction represents a smaller quantity. They also covered the concept of fractional units, where one unit is divided into equal parts, and unit fractions, where the numerator is always 1. The session concluded with practice questions about weighing guas and packing rice, with Mohammed demonstrating understanding of the concepts through his responses.

Fraction Problem Solution Discussion
Akash and Mohammed discussed solving a fraction problem involving two fish, where one fish weighs half a kg and the other weighs one-fourth of a kg. They determined that together the fish weigh three-fourths of a kg (3/4 kg) by comparing the parts of the whole represented by the fractions. Akash explained the concept using diagrams to show how half and one-fourth relate to the whole circle, with three parts out of four making up the total weight.

Fractions in Indian Languages
Akash discussed the historical use of fractions in India, mentioning that the concept of fractions like 3/4 was referred to as "tripada" in ancient times and can be found in modern Indian languages. He asked Mohammed to research and document how different fractions are referred to in his own language, including terms for one-fourth, half, three-quarters, and other common fractions. Akash also explained the relationship between different units of measurement like kilo and pound, and how fractions are expressed in these systems.
